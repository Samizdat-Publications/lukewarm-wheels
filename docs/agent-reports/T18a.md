# T18a — lobe stability

Status: **root cause found and fixed; the secondary goal (3 consecutive laps) is NOT met.**
Cars no longer leave the track. What is left is an energy/tuning problem (stalling), not a
stability problem. Owner of the remainder: T18.

## Root cause

There was no single bug. The "car leaves the lobe mid-arc" symptom was produced by four
independent defects that all fire within the first 0.4 s of a launch; the trace in HANDOFF.md
pointed at the lobe only because that is where the car happened to land. In order of severity:
(1) the foam nip's sideways push was applied 0.5 cm **below** the centre of mass
(`addForceAtPoint`), so a 1.1 N lateral force on a 0.15 cm lever arm acting on a 33 g·cm² roll
inertia spun the car about its roll axis to 188 rad/s *inside the nip*, before it ever reached a
curve. (2) The floor was a `trimesh` of 0.25 cm strip triangles with no `FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES`
flag, so a car crossing it at 300 cm/s caught on internal edges and took several-hundred-cm/s
ghost impulses whose contact normal was the edge direction and which left no manifold behind —
this is what made the failure look random and unattributable. (3) The lobe walls were selected
by `seg.lift > 0`, so every "flat arc" experiment silently dropped them from 2.6 cm to 0.9 cm,
shorter than the casting, which is why the arc appeared to fail even when flat; and the hard
0.9 → 2.6 cm height step left the taller wall's end cap standing square across the lane.
(4) The wall gap at each `#` crossing was cut from `inCrossing(laneCentre)` — a 9.8 cm hole in
both walls — and the flared lane mouths added to fix (3) were built into that hole, where they
sat exactly on the *other* circuit's lane centreline. Underneath all of it, a 20.46 cm lobe at
300 cm/s is a 4.5 g corner: the car must ride the outer wall, so the wall has to be taller than
the car and the cornering load has to be shared with a banked channel — `bankMaxDeg` was
specified in SPEC §5.2 but had never been implemented.

## Measured

Lone Porsche 959, `car.spawnAtGate('N-out')` after 0.5 s of motor spin-up, 240 Hz synchronous:

| metric | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| leaves the track | yes, every run, by t = 0.10 s | **no** — max roll (car up vs lane up) 20°, `offTrack` false through 40 s |
| launch speed out of the nip | 300–330 cm/s, then tumbling | 317 cm/s on the standing launch, 399 cm/s peak on a rolling re-boost |
| lobe apex reached | never (left the arc around s = 90) | every time |
| laps | 0 (one lucky lap, once) | **2** — lap 1 at t = 2.67 s, lap 2 at t = 6.74 s, then stalls at s ≈ 14 |
| five-car line-up | cars tumbled off the table | 0 cars fall off (min y = 0.98 cm), 0 crashes — all five stall in the hub |
| `vehicleMode: 'sled'` control | flips out | still flips out at t = 0.19 s (see below) |

Lap time 2.67 s for a 347 cm circuit = 130 cm/s average against a 317 cm/s launch: the car
spends most of a lap coasting down. Energy audit (specific energy `v²/2 + g·h`, cm²/s²):
51 000 leaving the nip → 30 000 by the end of the junction turn → ~0 by the time it reaches the
next nip 150 cm later. That is a mean retarding force of ~0.23 g, roughly half chassis scrub on
the outer wall and half the raycast vehicle's lateral wheel scrub.

## Remaining issues (hand to T18)

1. **Stalling, not instability.** The car is about 10 % short of the energy needed to lap
   indefinitely. It reliably dies either in the 17 cm dead zone between the two nips on one hub
   straight, or a few cm short of a nip. A 108-point grid over lift / bank / friction / gear
   ratio found nothing better than 2 laps. Next levers, in order: raise the launch ceiling (the
   no-load foam surface speed is 400 cm/s but the motor bogs and the standing launch only
   reaches 317 — look at `foamWheelMassG` and `motorJ`); widen `boostExtra` to the true nip
   chord (≈1.46 cm for a 2.45 cm casting, currently 1.2); reduce the junction turn's share of
   the loss further.
2. **`vehicleMode: 'sled'` is broken** — it flips out of the lobe at t = 0.19 s. It has no
   suspension, so nothing holds it down when the wall reaction acts above its c.o.m. Either drop
   the mode or give the sled its own (much lower) `comDrop`.
3. **Footprint drifted to 140 cm** (was 131) because `junctionRadius` 25 pushes the lobes
   outward. `curveRadius` is still 20.46. If 131 cm is load-bearing, re-solve `straightLen` /
   `hubHalf` against it.
4. **`lobeLift` is now 2 cm, not the 9 cm in HANDOFF.** 9 cm costs 8 800 cm²/s² of specific
   energy per lobe, which the car does not have. Either the real lift is much smaller than the
   estimate or the drag model is too lossy — resolve against RESEARCH.md before shipping.
5. **No crashes in `lineUpFive`** — cars now reach the crossing too slowly to exceed
   `crashSpeed` 60. This should return once (1) is fixed; the crash detection itself is fine.
6. **`lengthUnit` is 1, not 100.** See the Lessons note in HANDOFF.md.
