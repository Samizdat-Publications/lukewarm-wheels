# T11 — `src/50-render-scene.js` (scene renderer)

Owner: Opus subagent. Date: 2026-09-08. Status: **done**.

## What it builds

`HW.render = { init, update, setCamera, xray, pickLane, cameraNames, scene, camera,
renderer, controls, selectedCar }` — `90-main.js` picks it over `HW.debugRender`
automatically. One file, 460 lines, no per-frame allocation (all `THREE.Vector3` /
`Quaternion` / `Matrix4` / `Raycaster` scratch objects are module-level singletons).

- **Renderer / lights.** WebGLRenderer, `ACESFilmicToneMapping` at exposure 1.18,
  shadows behind `HW.config.shadows` (`PCFShadowMap` — `PCFSoftShadowMap` is
  deprecated in three r185 and logs a warning). Hemisphere + warm key directional
  with a shadow camera sized from `HW.sim.track.bounds` (±(span·0.62+12)), plus a
  cool fill. A 900 cm floor plane at **y = −4** with a procedural canvas
  laminate/plank texture (30× repeat).
- **Track.** `mesh.floorVisual` and `mesh.walls` as `BufferGeometry` with computed
  normals, orange `0xff6a00` / darker `0xd44f00` plastic, both `DoubleSide`.
  Grey posts + feet at each `mesh.supports` apex, and one `InstancedMesh` of short
  connector feet every 22 cm along the flat track so the ribbons do not float.
- **Hub.** Red plates from `mesh.hub.plates`, a crossed red module body under them
  (y −3.6 … −0.45), the four `mesh.hub.housings` split into two pieces around the
  foam wheel plus a low spine running under it, dark module faces at the arm tips,
  a battery box under the west arm and an ON/OFF slide switch on the south face
  whose knob slides with `telemetry.elec.on`.
- **Foam wheels.** Grey cylinders at `track.wheels[i].center`, radius `.radius`,
  height 1.6, two dark radial marks, a spindle down into the module. Spun by
  `HW.sim.elec.omegaWheel * dt`.
- **Gear train.** Motor can + pinion on the north module, a central idler and four
  satellites coaxial with the foam wheels — each a cylinder plus one `InstancedMesh`
  of teeth. Pinion spins at `telemetry.elec.rpmMotor`, satellites and idler at
  `rpmWheel`; satellites all one way, idler and pinion the other.
  `xray(true)` drops the shared hub material to opacity 0.25 (and turns off its
  shadow casting) and switches on a point light under the module.
- **Cars.** `HW.renderCars.buildMesh(car.entry)` when present, else a fallback
  box casting copied from `90-main.js`. Per frame: position/quaternion from
  `car.pos/quat`, `visible = !car.lifted`, wheel spin from `car.wheelState(k)`
  (`rotation.x`) and suspension travel from
  `controller.wheelChassisConnectionPointCs(k).y - ws.len`, then
  `HW.renderCars.sync(car, group)` if it exists.
- **Cameras.** `overview | crash | chase | booster | gears`. OrbitControls (with
  damping) on all but `chase`, which lerps a position 14 cm behind and 4 cm above
  `HW.render.selectedCar || HW.sim.cars[0]`. `gears` also turns x-ray on.
- **`pickLane(ndcX, ndcY)`** raycasts the visual track floor, projects the hit onto
  both circuits and returns `{path, s, lateral}` for the nearer one when
  `dist < 4`, else `null`.
- **Crash sparks.** `HW.bus.on('crash')` fires one of 6 pooled `THREE.Points`
  bursts of 12 additive sprites at `e.at`, gravity-integrated, fading over 0.4 s.
  Positions/velocities are preallocated.
- Resize handler on `window`.

## Verified in the browser (`http://localhost:8765/`, three r185, Rapier 0.20.0)

- **Zero console errors and zero warnings** after boot + `lineUpFive()` + switch on.
- All five presets set without throwing; screenshots in `docs/screenshots/`:
  `01-2026-09-08-first-render.png` (1600×1000), `05-2026-09-08-first-crash.jpg`, `T11-booster.jpg`,
  `02-2026-09-08-gear-train-xray.jpg` (gear train legible through the 25 %-opaque hub),
  `T11-chase.jpg`.
- `pickLane`: projecting a known lane point (circuit NS, s = 40) to NDC and picking
  it back returns `{path: 'NS', s: 40.06, lateral: −0.15}`; an off-track NDC
  returns `null`.
- `xray(true/false)` toggles `hubMat.opacity` 0.25 ↔ 1 and `transparent` true ↔ false.
- Gear directions with the motor running (ωwheel = 6.22 rad/s, 387 motor rpm) over
  a 0.1 s update: foam wheel +0.6218, satellite +0.6218, idler −0.6218, pinion
  −4.0505 → pinion:satellite ratio **−6.51**, i.e. `gearRatio` 6.5 with the
  correct reversal.
- 335 draw calls, 112 k triangles, RAF-uncapped frame rate ~187 fps headless.
- All five car groups expose `userData.wheels` with 4 meshes; T12's real castings
  render through `HW.renderCars.buildMesh` when that file is loaded.

## Contract deviations (also added to SPEC §5.9)

1. `pickLane(ndcX, ndcY)` takes two scalars (SPEC §6 wrote `pickLane(ndc)`); this
   matches `60-ui.js`, which already calls it with two arguments.
2. The room floor is at y = −4 cm and the set stands on short grey connector feet,
   so the red hub module (y −3.6 … −0.45) has room for the gear train and battery
   box. The lane surfaces stay at y = 0 exactly as the physics builds them.
3. The **visual** floor ribbon of the second circuit is lifted 0.03 cm, because the
   NS and EW ribbons are exactly coplanar where they cross the hub and z-fight.
   The offset is applied to a copy of `mesh.floorVisual.positions`; colliders and
   `HW.sim.mesh` are untouched. The code validates the vertex layout
   (4 verts/sample per circuit) and no-ops if 31-track-mesh ever changes it.
4. Wheel-mesh contract for `51-render-cars.js`: `group.userData.wheels =
   [FL, FR, RL, RR]` (the `42-vehicle.js` order), each built with its axle along
   local X (`rotation.z = PI/2`) so `rotation.x` is the spin angle. The renderer
   writes spin and suspension travel first and then calls
   `HW.renderCars.sync(car, group)`, which may override.

## Not done / left for later

- No `HW.render.rebuild()`. `60-ui.js` looks for one and falls back to a page
  reload when the tuning drawer changes geometry; that fallback is fine but a
  rebuild would be nicer (rebuild the track/hub meshes in place after
  `HW.sim.create()`).
- Sparks are the only effect: no motion blur, no skid marks, no motor-whine hook.
- The x-ray fades the hub only. The track ribbons stay opaque, so the gear-train
  camera looks up from under the table rather than through the lanes.
- Foam wheels are plain grey cylinders (per the brief); a foam-texture normal map
  would sell the material better.
- Draw calls (335) could drop by merging the hub boxes into one geometry if the
  Showroom (T15) adds pressure.
